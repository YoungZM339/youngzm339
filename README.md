I build practical products across web, desktop, backend, machine-learning, and infrastructure workflows. My repositories include research platforms, campus and business applications, algorithm visualizers, browser extensions, and engineering experiments.

## Engineering principles

- Document the real system rather than advertising features that do not exist.
- Treat security, privacy, data licensing, and operational recovery as part of product design.
- Prefer reproducible environments, explicit configuration, and testable interfaces.
- Separate prototypes and course demonstrations from production-readiness claims.
- Preserve upstream attribution and licensing when building on open-source foundations.
